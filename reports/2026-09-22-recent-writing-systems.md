# Recent writing systems: September 22, 2026

Scope: substantive public updates from **August 23 through September 22, 2026**, inclusive. This refresh adds three narrow safeguards and clearer evaluation reporting. It does not establish a twofold improvement in writing quality or concision.

## What changed

- **Keep essential qualifications with claims.** A result must remain interpretable without opening its appendix. Put the qualification beside the claim; supporting detail may move elsewhere.
- **Keep voice through the edit.** Editing inherits the current brief, approved samples, and protected wording. An edit operation must not silently replace the writer's voice with a generic style.
- **Protect the current draft.** Verify the document, scope, freshness, and completeness before replacing source text. An interrupted output is not an instruction to delete the rest. Use a focused patch or recoverable original when modifying files.
- **Separate drafting and editing evaluations.** Their failure modes differ. Do not turn stylometric features or detector scores into quality goals.

The core skill grew by one sentence. Conditional file-edit guidance stays in the workflow reference. The previous role/audience/output brief remains; names of discussion participants were removed from current documentation. No commercial writing service supplies a dependency or universal brevity formula.
